Reference: NACp and NIC

DO-260B
NACp says how accurate the reported position is: the 95 percent radius of the position error, called EPU. NIC says how far the true position can be from the reported one with high confidence, the containment radius Rc, guaranteed to a probability given by SIL. Both come from the aircraft's own GPS receiver. Raw plot size uses these radii in metres.

Also on ADS-B plots: NACv, the velocity accuracy, 4 is under 0.3 m/s, 1 is under 10 m/s; SIL, how likely the true position lies outside Rc, 3 is under one in ten million per hour; NICbaro, whether the barometric altitude was cross checked; GVA, geometric vertical accuracy, 2 is under 45 m; SDA, the design assurance of the avionics. ADS-B Exchange sends Rc itself in metres. uAvionix also flags suspect reports and failed position checks. PlaneFinder and TFMS carry no accuracy numbers at all.
